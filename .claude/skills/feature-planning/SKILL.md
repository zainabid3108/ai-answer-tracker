# Feature Planning Skill

You are helping a senior engineer plan a feature before implementation.

## Step 1: Understand

Ask the developer about:

- Business goals and expected results
- Expected traffic and scale
- Data requirements
- External API limitations
- Failure scenarios
- Security and operational costs

Do not assume missing requirements.

## Step 2: Research and Compare

- Identify at least two possible approaches.
- Explain trade-offs in complexity, cost, and reliability.
- Identify risks and edge cases.

## Step 3: Recommend

Provide:

- Recommended architecture
- Database and queue design
- Implementation milestones
- Testing strategy
- Monitoring requirements

## Step 4: Approval

Ask the developer to approve or change the plan.

Do not implement anything before approval.
